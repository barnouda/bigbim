# BIGBIM = Bayesian Inversion of Gravimetric data and Bayesian Inversion of Magnetic data

## Overview

These codes perform Bayesian linear inversion of gravimetric and magnetic data to retreive 3D density or magnetization models.

## Where is the code?

The BIG and BIM codes are available on Codeberg: https://codeberg.org/barnouda/bigbim

## Author

Anne Barnoud - 2013/2016
